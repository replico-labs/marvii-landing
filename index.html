Security

Calm by design

  1. 01

    Passkeys only work on Marvii's own sites, near.marvii.xyz and wallet.marvii.xyz, so a fake site can't use them.

  2. 02

    Agents never hold your key. Their own keys are boxed in by on-chain rules, and can live inside a secure hardware enclave.

  3. 03

    Built on open-source smart-account contracts: Kernel by ZeroDev on EVM chains and NEAR's passkey wallet contract on NEAR, with Swig on Solana next. Other chains are signed through NEAR's Chain Signatures network.

Questions

What if I lose my phone?+

Your passkey syncs through iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager. A second passkey and guardian recovery are coming.

Which phones work?+

iPhone with Face ID or Touch ID. Android 9 or later with Google Play Services and a screen lock.

Is it custodial?+

No. Only your passkey can control your wallet. Agents and apps get limited keys you can revoke.

What can agents do today?+

On testnet: swap on Uniswap, lend on Aave, trade perps on Hyperliquid, pay saved addresses on Bitcoin, Litecoin, Bitcoin Cash and Zcash, and vote and stake in DAOs through Protean. Anything outside their limits is refused or waits for your tap.

Can I bring my own agent?+

Yes. Any program that can sign with an ed25519 key can ask for a session. The developer guide shows the request format.

Does it cost anything?+

Not during the testnet beta.

Which chains?+

On testnet today, one account reaches NEAR, nine EVM chains (Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, BNB Chain, Polygon, Avalanche, Monad and HyperEVM), Bitcoin, Solana, TRON, XRP, Cardano, Litecoin, Dogecoin, Bitcoin Cash and Zcash.
