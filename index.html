Questions

What if I lose my phone?+

Your passkey syncs through iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager. A second passkey and guardian recovery are coming.

Which phones work?+

iPhone with Face ID or Touch ID. Android 9 or later with Google Play Services and a screen lock.

Is it custodial?+

No. Only your passkey can control your wallet.

Does it cost anything?+

Not during the testnet beta.

Which chains?+

Base, Monad, HyperEVM, Solana and NEAR testnets.
